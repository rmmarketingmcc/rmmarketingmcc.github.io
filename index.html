RM Activity

Mac appEnglish

See where your day actually goes: time per app, site and client, and real meeting time, counted from the microphone.

Your data: never leaves your Mac. No account, no analytics.

See RM ActivityGitHub

Sunscreen

Mac menu bar appEnglish

Your screens follow the sun: no blue light in the evening and a brightness that fits the hour, on every screen you plug in.

Your data: stays on your Mac. It checks GitHub for updates, makes one anonymous count a day, and asks Apple for your place name only if you share your location.

See SunscreenGitHub

Sales Coach

Mac appDanish

A coach that sits in on your sales calls, on your own Mac, without a bot in the meeting: talk time, a checklist, cues while you talk, and notes afterwards.

Your data: audio never leaves your Mac. With the AI on, only the text goes to your own Claude.

See Sales CoachGitHub

Case Carousel

For your websiteDanish

Show your cases like a pro: a free, self-contained carousel in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No plugins, nothing to install.

Your data: nothing to send. It loads nothing from anywhere else.

See Case CarouselGitHub

How we build them

The same rules for every tool, so you can trust what you put on your Mac or your site.

Open sourceEvery line is on GitHub under the MIT license. Read it, change it, make it yours.
No accountsNone of them asks you to sign up, and we run no servers for them.
Clear about dataEach page says exactly what leaves your device: RM Activity, Sunscreen, Sales Coach.
Help when it breaksReport a problem right from the apps. It opens a report on GitHub with the technical details filled in, and you see all of it before you send it.
